# Brand and voice

## Positioning

The brand is not selling "more AI". It is selling **judgment, independence, and safer learning in a world where AI is already present**.

The parent-facing emotional truth: my child is probably going to use AI anyway; I do not want AI doing the thinking for them; I also do not want them left behind; I want them to learn to use the tool without becoming dependent on it.

## Voice

Write like a thoughtful teacher or parent, not a technology company. Prefer short concrete sentences, specific observations, plain verbs, calm confidence, honest uncertainty, and encouragement tied to effort or a specific step.

Good examples: "That one was hard." "You got the first step right." "Want a hint, or another try first?" "I'm not sure about that source. Let's check it." "Try this one without me."

Avoid generic praise such as "Amazing job! You're a brilliant learner!"

## Avoid AI-sounding marketing language

Do not use: empower learners; unlock potential; transformative learning; seamless experience; personalized learning journey; navigate the AI landscape; leverage AI; harness the power of AI; future-ready learners; revolutionary education.

Also avoid repetitive slogan stacks, excessive em dashes, constant exclamation marks, and grids of interchangeable feature cards.

## Warmth without anthropomorphism

The robot may be visually friendly, but must not be positioned as a friend, companion, confidant, or replacement parent/teacher. The child remains the active learner. The robot should point, prompt, or observe; it should not do the child's work.

## Ethical marketing

No countdown timers, fake scarcity, streak-loss mechanics, manipulative notifications, "best friend" framing, fearmongering, or unqualified claims of safety/guaranteed outcomes.

## Visual character

Editorial rather than gamified. Suggested palette: Forest `#173A2B`, Sage `#7C9275`, Soft sage `#DDE5D8`, Cream `#F7F2E9`, Paper `#FCFAF6`, Sand `#E8D8C6`, Peach `#E5A27C`, Ink `#17201B`, Muted ink `#566159`.

Use an editorial serif for large headlines and clean system sans-serif for UI/body.
