# Homepage build specification

Keep the homepage intentionally small: header, hero, market-gap evidence, three learning principles, independence statement/parent CTA, footer. Do not add pricing tables, testimonials, comparison matrices, FAQ accordions, dashboards, or ten-feature grids.

## Header

Desktop: wordmark left, maximum three nav links plus one restrained CTA. Mobile: wordmark left and accessible menu button right; Escape closes the menu.

## Hero

Eyebrow: `AI WILL BE PART OF THEIR WORLD.`

Headline line 1: `They're going to use AI.`

Headline line 2: `Help them learn how to use it well.`

Body: `A learning tool that explains, questions, and practices with kids — then gradually steps out of the way.`

Primary CTA: `See how it works`; secondary: `For parents`.

Required asset: `/assets/hero-child-robot.svg`. It must show the child writing on paper beside a small robot tutor. The child is doing the work.

### Desktop

1180–1240px max content width; two-column hero; generous whitespace; maximum two hero CTAs.

### Tablet

Stacking is allowed, but the hero image remains large and near the opening copy.

### Mobile — hard requirement

The image must not be hidden. It appears before the market-gap section; top edge within 650px at 390×844; width at least 320px; height at least 210px; use an intentional crop, not a tiny thumbnail.

## Market gap

Headline: `AI is already here. Guidance isn't.`

`86%` — `of U.S. children ages 9–17 surveyed in 2026 reported using AI`.

Supporting fact: `More than 4 in 10 surveyed children said no parent or guardian had ever talked with them about AI safety.`

Source must be visibly linked to Common Sense Media and include age/year context.

## Three principles

Only three and compact: **Ask** — Start with the question. **Think** — Get a hint, not the answer. **Try alone** — Practice without help.

## Independence statement

Use: `Sometimes the best thing our AI can say is: “Try this one without me.”`

## Responsive design

Must be visually complete at 1440×1000, 834×1112, and 390×844 with no horizontal overflow from 320px to 1600px.
